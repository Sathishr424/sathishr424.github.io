What it is

A price-tracking website that answers one question for shoppers: is this a good price? It shows a live deals feed ranked by discount, filters by category, store, discount and price, and a product page with every store's current price, the all-time low, a price history chart and past sales.

My part

I built most of the Vue.js UI and the Laravel backend, including the full product page, and about 90% of the scrapers. The site is still live and runs largely on my implementation.

Keeping prices live

  • One scraper per retailer, run on cron schedules that store every price check
  • Rotating proxies so no single IP gets rate-limited or blocked
  • Live CAPTCHA solving when a site challenges a request
  • Headless browsers for retailers that render prices with JavaScript

From prices to deals

Every check is stored as a snapshot. From those, the backend works out each product's daily low across stores, its all-time and 30-day lows, each store's sale runs, and the deals feed.
