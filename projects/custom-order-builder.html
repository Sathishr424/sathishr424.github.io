Custom Order Builder

A 2-step product builder for a custom-apparel business that prices bulk orders live, so customers skip the quote.

The problem

Custom apparel is usually sold by quote: the customer sends a logo, waits for a price and goes back and forth. The client wanted customers to configure their order and see the exact price themselves, from one item to hundreds.

What I built

  • Step 1: choose colors and enter quantities per size. Sold-out sizes are locked and a running total updates as you type.
  • Step 2: choose the decoration type and placement, upload a logo or enter text, and add extras.
  • A pricing engine with 10 quantity tiers, a price table per decoration type, tiered add-ons, a small-order fee and the savings shown against the single-item price.
  • A Laravel and MySQL backend for pricing, artwork uploads and orders, and an admin panel where the owners review every order.

How it fits together

The storefront and checkout run on Shopify. The builder hands the configured order to the Shopify cart, while the Laravel backend keeps the pricing rules, artwork and order details behind it.
