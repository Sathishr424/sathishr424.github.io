The problem

Retailers, resellers and catalog teams needed product data from other stores: whole catalogs with prices, specs and images, in a format they could import. Copying it by hand would take months, and every site is built differently.

What I built

A custom Python scraper for each store:

  • Finding products by walking category pages or sitemaps, or from the client's own URL or SKU list.
  • Fetching pages with Requests and BeautifulSoup for static sites, and Selenium with undetected Chrome for dynamic sites, infinite scroll, reCAPTCHA and heavily protected stores. Threads, proxies and rotating user agents kept big runs fast, and failed URLs were logged and retried.
  • Extracting fields: titles, SKUs, brands, categories, prices, sale prices and rebates, options, stock, shipping, specs as clean HTML, manuals, videos and images.
  • Delivering CSV or Excel files in the client's column format, with images downloaded and named to match.

Scale

The largest jobs were 77k+ products from one tools retailer, 56k+ and 38k+ from others, and 140k+ product URLs crawled on a single site. The smallest were brand sites with under 100 products.
